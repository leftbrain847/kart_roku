KART for Roku

A fake-3D kart race for the TV: three laps of a curving course against three bot racers. Five lanes, and every lane is its own track. Where the road splits, tap Left or Right to jump the gap to the other track. Grab item blocks and hit the karts ahead with shells.

Download

Download kart_roku.zip
  • Published only after the package security check passes
  • Game code and images only: no network calls, no store or account access
  • Scanned for keys and tokens before every release
  • Unsigned developer build: you install it yourself in Roku developer mode

Source and release notes: github.com/leftbrain847/kart_roku/releases

Put it on your Roku

  1. On the Roku remote press Home ×3, Up ×2, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right. Turn on developer mode, set a password, and note the address it shows.
  2. On a computer or phone on the same Wi-Fi, open that address (for example http://192.168.1.20) and sign in as rokudev with your password.
  3. Upload kart_roku.zip and press Install. Kart starts on the TV.

Controls

Left / Right
Change lane; across a split it jumps to the other track
Up
Throw your shell or banana forward
Down
Drop it behind you
OK
Use a boost
Play/Pause
Pause menu: resume, traffic on/off, restart, exit
Back
Exit the game

Three split zones per lap (2|3, 1|3|1, 3|2) that rejoin after a stretch. Jumping clears bananas. Speed carries through splits. Best lap is saved on the Roku.

From the simulator

Frames rendered by the brs-cli BrightScript simulator during the automated test race. Built for the Roku Streaming Stick HD (3840R): roScreen with Draw2D, sprites pre-scaled once at startup.
